# Simple Bank Fund Transfer

## Project Requirement

Customers should be able to transfer money from their savings account to another Simple Bank account using the bank's web app.

## Transfer Rules

1. The transfer amount must be greater than Rs 0.
2. The sender must have enough balance to complete the transfer.
3. A customer can transfer at most Rs 1,00,000 per day.
4. A customer cannot transfer money to the same account.
5. Both the sender's and recipient's accounts must be active.
6. Every transfer must have a unique transaction ID and be saved.
7. The customer should receive a quick response.
